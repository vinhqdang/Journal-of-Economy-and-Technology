## Manuscript proposals

Two candidate ideas for submission to the Journal of Economy and Technology. Both sit in the economics of access to generative AI.

| Proposal | Type | Core question | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| [A: Regional pricing and generative AI adoption](proposals/A_regional_pricing_causal_effects.md) | Empirical, quasi-experimental | What do localized low-price tiers and free promotions do to adoption in lower-income countries? | Noisy proxy outcomes, few treated clusters |
| [B: Who pays for proof?](proposals/B_verifiable_billing_market.md) | Theory with calibration | Which institutions make honest billing of hidden LLM usage an equilibrium, and who bears the cost? | Thin novelty margin against existing micro-level models |

Suggested order: A first, because its data collection is time-sensitive (a promotion in India expires in November 2026). B second.
