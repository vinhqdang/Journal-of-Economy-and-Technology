# Human check of the extracted data

The author checked several random samples of the extracted data by hand against the source papers (reported 4 and 5 October 2026). The sizes of the samples and the corrections made were not logged, so the check is described but not quantified. Where the manuscript refers to it, it says so.

Any correction found by the author should be applied to `data/extraction.json` and `data/mra_estimates.csv` and the analyses rerun (`scripts/merge_extractions.py`, `scripts/apply_second_pass.py`, `scripts/mra_pilot.py`, `scripts/extra_questions.py`).
