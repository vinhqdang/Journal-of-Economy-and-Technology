# Pilot: within-study income contrasts (23 Nov 2026)

Data: `data/contrasts.json` (coded from the verified extraction records and 13 available full texts; not yet verified by a second reader). Analysis: `scripts/contrast_meta.py`, output in `contrast_pilot_output.txt`.

- 32 candidate studies; 27 have at least one richer-vs-poorer contrast (88 contrasts, 27 headline).
- Headline directions: richer larger 10, poorer larger 6, opposite signs 8, no clear difference 3. Sign test 10 vs 6, p = 0.45.
- Only 9 headline contrasts have t/SE and N for both groups (or for the interaction). Pooled difference in partial correlations (richer minus poorer): +0.010 [-0.047, +0.066], I2 33%.
- Reading: no detectable difference; the interval rules out large differences in either direction, but k is small.
- Needed: full texts of 18 studies to recover SE/t and N (1192, 138, 1580, 268, 535, 707, 879, 9001, 9101, 9102, 9103, 9105, 9201, 9204, 9501, 952, 9701, 9705), and a second independent coder.
